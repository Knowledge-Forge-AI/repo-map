## Boundaries

No complete local suite, local project coverage, application/SDK build, new
dependency, artifact/model substitution, corpus/label/query change, held-out
execution, body embedding, production pgvector/schema/index admission, live
deployment, main/tag/release promotion, PR mutation, automatic retry, manual
dispatch or duplicate trigger. The upstream Debian informational treatment,
workflow, recipe, hard coverage gates and historical debt remain unchanged.

Hosted CI owns complete-population qualification and coverage. Scoped evidence
and successful calibration do not establish learned usefulness, superiority,
generalization, full W5 completion or release acceptance.

## Failure and rollback

A refused, failed, canceled or timed-out campaign retains its exact reservation
and consumption history. Any successor needs fresh authority and bindings.
Rollback is a reviewed forward change preserving all published history and
historical evidence; no spent allocation or attempt directory is reset.
