## Boundaries

No local complete suite, local Python coverage, local application/SDK build,
optional model attempt, dependency or package/lockfile change, model/input
substitution, corpus/query/gold change, held-out execution, production pgvector,
schema/index admission, live deployment, main/tag/release promotion or history
rewrite. Existing upstream Debian informational treatment and base freshness
policy remain in force. Application context and SDK recipe must be recomputed;
evaluator-only changes are not evidence that either stayed unchanged.

Any failure, cancellation or timeout retains its exact reservation and observed
consumption. A successor requires fresh authority and bindings. Rollback is a
reviewed forward change; historical attempts and published commits remain.
