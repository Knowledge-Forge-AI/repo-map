“Ci passed carry on with roadmap,” and the standing direction to batch
roughly one to three epics before public staging. This allocates one fresh
candidate campaign after both ADR0162 epics and their scoped proof; it is
neither rearm nor automatic retry of CI124. Publication remains contingent

assembled-system and Rust evidence was independently reconciled against its

  No CI126, start37, rerun, second push, main/tag/release or deployment is

## Preserved gates and limits

Hard unit85/85 and integration80/80 coverage gates, branch mode, full source
denominator, suppressions, test population, timeouts, packaging, images and
dependency policy are unchanged. Desired 87/82 branch margins remain open;
CI124 left 55/425 branches at its denominator. New local scoped results make
no coverage-margin claim.

No complete local unit/integration/smoke/staging/system/combined population,
local Python coverage, image build, acquisition or native parser/model
execution is granted. Use existing cached sandbox images for exact grant
proof. The manifest and grant closure are source-bound; local checks do not
become hosted, image, installed, full W6/W7 or scientific acceptance.

The operator's 10k–100k meaningful-LOC aim guides batching. These two
connected correctness epics form the milestone even if their measured delta
is smaller; do not manufacture modules, fixtures or tests to reach a number.
Record the actual delta and proof selectors in the phase exit and commit.

## Rollback and outcome ownership

Before publication, retain exact preimages and candidate-bound evidence.
Do not rewrite reported history or delete shared source objects on refusal.
After publication, any regression receives a reviewed forward correction
under a new manual instruction. The parent owns final review disposition,
the single publication and honest pending hosted status. Existing P3 scope
and all unrelated release, production and installed decisions remain held.
