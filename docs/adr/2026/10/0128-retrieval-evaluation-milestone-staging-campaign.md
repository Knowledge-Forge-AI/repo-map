complete retained static enforcement, compile/topology/export/length/privacy

two-point margin above hard gates. Local checks do not qualify the new whole
candidate. Record it NOT ACCEPTED until fresh hosted evidence passes. An executed
failure requires diagnosis and new retry authority; no automatic rearm.

No public-main promotion, tag, repository rename, package-manager publication,
live deployment, W3 indexing or acceptance of additional supplier findings is
included. Rollback is a separately reviewed forward change; published history
and sealed namespace ancestry remain intact.
